Signature · 02

Made slowly. Savored slowly.

Every Choco truffle begins with cocoa beans chosen for character, not convenience. We roast, grind and temper in small batches so the flavor stays deep, round and unhurried. Then each piece is finished by hand, one at a time.

Midnight Dark Truffle

72% dark chocolate ganache with a cocoa-dusted shell. Deep, bittersweet, with a long finish of roasted cocoa and a hint of dried cherry.

Velvet Cocoa Dust

A soft milk-chocolate center rolled in fine cocoa powder. Creamy, gentle and quietly nostalgic.

Gold Leaf Praline

Hazelnut praline wrapped in dark chocolate and finished with a touch of edible gold. Crisp, nutty and a little dramatic.

Sea Salt Caramel

Slow-cooked salted caramel inside a glossy dark shell. Sweet first, then savory, then gone too soon.

Why Choco · 03

Chocolate with intention.

Pillar 01

Honest ingredients

Cocoa, cocoa butter, cream, sugar and real flavors. Nothing we cannot pronounce, nothing that hides the taste of the chocolate itself.

Pillar 02

Patient craft

Our truffles rest, set and mature before they leave the kitchen. Good chocolate cannot be rushed, so we do not rush it.

Pillar 03

Made to be shared

From a single truffle for yourself to a hand-tied box for someone you love, every piece is made to turn an ordinary moment into a small occasion.

The Method

How a Choco truffle comes to life.

01

Select

We source cocoa from small farms that harvest and ferment with care, ensuring pure origin character.

02

Craft

Beans are roasted, ground and tempered in small batches for a smooth, glossy finish and clean snap.

03

Finish

Each truffle is filled, dipped, dusted and inspected by hand before it is boxed for your enjoyment.
