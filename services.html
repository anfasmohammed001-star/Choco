Common Enquiries

Frequently asked questions.

How fresh are your truffles?
They are made in small batches and packed fresh. For the best taste, enjoy them within two weeks and store them in a cool, dry place.
Do you offer vegan or nut-free options?
We can prepare selected flavors without dairy or nuts on request. Please tell us about allergies when you order, as our kitchen handles nuts and milk.
Can I add my company logo?
Yes. We offer logo-stamped boxes and branded labels for corporate orders.
How early should I order for events?
We recommend at least two weeks for large orders and custom designs.
Do you deliver?
Yes. We deliver locally and can arrange courier delivery to other cities. Chocolate is packed to stay safe in warm weather.
Can I taste before I order?
Ask us about our tasting box, a small selection designed to help you choose.
