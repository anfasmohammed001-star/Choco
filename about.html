— Our Story · 01

Born from a love of dark chocolate.

Choco began with one simple idea: chocolate should taste like chocolate. Rich, deep and honest. We built our kitchen around that idea, and we have never moved away from it.

Origins

A small kitchen. A big obsession.

Choco started in a small kitchen with a stove, a marble slab and a stubborn belief that the best chocolate is made slowly. Long before the first box was sold, there were hundreds of test batches, endless tasting notes and a lot of burnt sugar.

We were not trying to make the sweetest chocolate or the flashiest. We wanted the kind that makes you stop talking for a second. The kind with a dark, velvety center and a finish that stays with you.

Today our kitchen is bigger, but the method is the same. Small batches, careful hands and a lot of patience.

Core Values

What we believe.

Pillar 01

Quality over quantity

We make fewer truffles so each one can be better. If a batch is not right, it does not leave the kitchen.

Pillar 02

Flavor before fashion

We do not chase trends. We refine classic flavors until they feel inevitable, then add a few surprises.

Pillar 03

Respect for the bean

Cocoa is a farmed crop with a story. We buy from growers who ferment and dry their beans with care, and we pay fairly for that work.

The Journey

From bean to truffle.

STEP 01

Sourcing

We choose cocoa for aroma, acidity and depth of flavor, not just price.

STEP 02

Roasting

Small roasts bring out notes of nuts, fruit and caramel without burning the bean.

STEP 03

Grinding & conching

Slow refining makes the chocolate silky and removes bitterness.

STEP 04

Tempering

Careful heating and cooling gives each shell its shine and clean snap.

STEP 05

Hand finishing

Filling, dipping, dusting and boxing are all done by hand.
